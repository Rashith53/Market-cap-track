    Recommended deployment

    How the recommendation works

    1. It starts with your current portfolio exposure.
    2. It calculates the effect of putting new money into each fund you already hold.
    3. It selects the fund mix that moves the overall portfolio closest to your target.
    4. If a category is underweight but is available only through a fund you already hold (for example Midcap through your Large & Midcap fund), that fund is recommended.

    Important: This calculator does not ask you to buy a new standalone Midcap fund. It works with your existing funds.

    About this price

    This card tries to fetch today's 22K gold & silver price for Tamil Nadu (Chennai reference) from Goodreturns automatically every time you open the app. When it works, you'll see "🟢 Live" above with a timestamp — genuinely no typing needed.

    Being straightforward about how fragile this is: a single HTML file can't directly read another website (browsers block that for security — it's called CORS), so this goes through a free third-party relay to get around it. That relay, or goodreturns.in changing their page layout, could break this at any time without warning. If that happens, the app falls back automatically to the last price it saw, and you can always tap "Enter price manually instead" to type in today's rate yourself from goodreturns.in, your jeweller, or MCX — that's saved on this device either way.

    How this works

    My Funds — this app doesn't fetch live fund data. You get the market-cap split (Nifty 50 / Next 50 / Midcap / Smallcap) straight from the fund's own factsheet or the AMC's website, and enter it once per fund. Tap "Edit" on any saved fund to change it later. The app does the multiplication and adds it all up across your holdings, so you can see your true index exposure in one place.
    Target allocation — set the mix you want (e.g. Nifty 50: 40%, Next 50: 30%, Midcap: 20%, Smallcap: 10%). The app compares that to what you actually hold right now — within your current total amount — and shows, category by category, whether you're short and need to add, or overweight and should reduce.
    Import your holdings — upload your Groww Excel (.xlsx) holdings statement and the app automatically finds the real holdings table (skipping the personal-details and summary rows at the top), reads the fund name and current value, and pre-fills a typical allocation % using both the fund name and Groww's own category column. You review every row before anything is added, and can still edit any fund afterward.
    Where to find the %: search "[fund name] factsheet" or "[fund name] portfolio market cap allocation" — every AMC publishes this monthly, usually as Large Cap / Mid Cap / Small Cap weights. Nifty 50 ≈ top 50 companies (large cap), Next 50 ≈ the 51st–100th largest, Midcap 250 ≈ roughly 101st–350th.
    Gold vs Silver — automatically fetches today's 22K gold & silver price for Tamil Nadu (Chennai reference) from Goodreturns each time you open the app, and works out the ratio against the standard bands Indian investors use (below 50 = gold cheap, above 80 = silver cheap). This relies on a third-party relay to get around browser security (CORS), so it's best-effort — if it fails, the app falls back to the last price it saw and you can enter today's rate yourself under "Enter price manually instead," which is saved on this device too. Formula: (gold price per gram) ÷ (silver price per gram). It's a general rule of thumb for comparing relative value, not a prediction or personalised advice.
    Your data stays on this device, automatically. Your fund list and the last gold/silver price you entered are saved right in this phone's browser storage — nothing is sent to any server, and you don't need to do anything to save it. Clearing your browser's site data/history will erase it, so use the backup button on the Funds tab if you want a copy you can restore, or move it to another phone.

    Add it to your home screen

    Open this app in your phone's browser (Chrome or Safari), then:

    Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen" → Add.

    iPhone (Safari): tap the Share icon → "Add to Home Screen" → Add.

    It'll then sit on your home screen with its own icon and open full-screen like a regular app — no browser bar.

    Want to share this app?

    This is a single file — index.html. Anyone can open it by double-tapping it, no install needed, and then add it to their own home screen the same way. To share a real link instead of a file:
    1. Create a free GitHub account, make a new repository, and upload this file as index.html.
    2. In the repository's Settings → Pages, set the source to the main branch.
    3. GitHub gives you a link like yourname.github.io/reponame — share that with anyone.
